About

Why this exists.

KayWorks

A place for the small apps I make and decide are worth keeping around.

I like making software in my free time. Usually it starts with something pretty ordinary: I want a better way to keep track of something, an existing app is doing way more than I need, or I think a small annoyance could be handled better.

Once one of those projects gets useful enough, I clean it up and keep it here. The goal is not to turn every idea into a product. It is just nice to make something that works well, keep using it, and let other people use it too if it happens to fit what they need.

The kind of software I like

I tend to prefer apps that open quickly, explain themselves, and do not ask for a bunch of setup before you can use them. If something can work without an account, I would rather not make you create one. If the data can stay on your device, that is usually where I want it.

That does not mean every app has to be tiny or stripped down. Money Tracker has grown into a pretty serious project. I just try to make the complexity earn its place instead of adding it because that is what software is “supposed” to have.

Why the name?

KayWorks is simply the umbrella for these projects. It gives them one home, one general design language, and a place to explain what is behind them without pretending they are all part of some giant software company.

I am not trying to sell a lifestyle here. I just like making useful things, and this is a clean place to keep them.
