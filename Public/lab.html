In development

Workout / Fitness Tracker

I want workout logging to feel less like filling out paperwork. The main idea is that you can paste a plan or type messy notes and let AI turn that into structured exercises, sets, reps, weight, and sessions.

The app itself will still own the real math: volume, progression, personal records, workout frequency, and history. Nutrition may fit later, but I am not counting it as a feature yet.

In development

Ascend

Ascend is a climbing tracker I originally made as a student. I want to revisit it now with a cleaner interface, better history, and the same local-first approach I use for newer KayWorks apps.

The direction includes sessions, climbs, grades, attempts, sends, flashes, projects, and progression. The old project is the starting point, not something I am just reskinning.

What comes after these?

No giant roadmap. If another little app solves a problem well enough that I keep using it, it will probably end up here.

No fake launch dates

These are side projects. I would rather say “still working on it” than put a date on something just to make the page look more official.
